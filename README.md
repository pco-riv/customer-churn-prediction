# Customer Churn Prediction

## Overview

End-to-end machine learning project focused on predicting customer churn for a telecommunications company.

## Business Problem

The company wants to identify customers who are at high risk of leaving the service and understand the main factors associated with customer churn.

## Objectives

- Predict the probability of customer churn.
- Identify the main factors associated with churn.
- Compare different machine learning models.
- Provide actionable business insights.
